PERM Process Guide

An overview of the Department of Labor PERM process.

PERM labor certification at a glance

PERM is the Department of Labor's labor certification process used by many employment-based immigrant cases. It generally concerns whether there are able, willing, qualified, and available U.S. workers for the offered position and whether employing the worker affects similarly employed U.S. workers' wages and conditions.

The employer is responsible for the labor certification process. Recruitment and prevailing-wage requirements apply, and cases may be selected for audit or otherwise require additional review. Requirements and case circumstances vary; this guide is informational, not legal advice.

The public FLAG processing-time page reports aggregate queue and average-processing indicators. It does not expose a person's current case stage or guarantee a case completion date.

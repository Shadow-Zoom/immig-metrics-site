How to read the Department of State Visa Bulletin charts.

How to read Visa Bulletin cutoffs

The Department of State publishes monthly Visa Bulletin charts, including Final Action Dates and Dates for Filing. Availability depends on preference category, chargeability area, and the chart relevant to the benefit being requested.

A priority date earlier than a listed cutoff can satisfy one date comparison, but it does not by itself establish eligibility. USCIS announces which chart adjustment-of-status applicants may use for a given month.

The official source is unavailable to this project at present. Any visible sample values are fictional and must not be used for case decisions.

Privacy

Immigration Metrics privacy practices.

Your information and this site

No account is required. Filing dates and priority dates entered in the tools remain in the page unless you explicitly check the corresponding “Remember” option. When checked, that date is stored in this browser's localStorage on this device only; it is not sent to Immigration Metrics or used for server-side case lookup.

Use “Clear saved preferences” to remove stored dates from this browser. Clearing site data in your browser also removes them. Do not use shared devices if you do not want locally remembered dates to remain available to another device user.

The site links to government websites. Those sites may receive standard connection information when you choose to visit them and are governed by their own privacy policies. This site may use browser storage only for the explicit date-remembering preference; it has no analytics or advertising integration in this implementation.

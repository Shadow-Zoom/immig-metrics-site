Why there is no decision-date estimate

The verified DOL snapshot has only one published average-duration observation (Analyst Review: 303 calendar days for August 2026). It is not a representative case cohort and does not provide distributions, percentiles, audit duration, or historical variance. A personal estimate or confidence interval would be unsupported.

The DOL queue month is an aggregate queue indicator—not your case's current stage or queue position. For a case-specific status, use the official FLAG case status search ↗.
